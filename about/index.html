Who We Are

Crafting Elegance
Since 2013

Evoca Fashion was born from a simple belief — every man deserves to look his finest, whether it's a weekday morning or the most important day of his life. Starting with a single showroom in Cherpulassery, we have grown into one of Kerala's most trusted names in men's fashion.

From premium shirts and daily wear to bespoke wedding sherwanis and rental coats, we bring together curated style, exceptional fabric quality, and personalized service under one roof — across four locations in Palakkad and Malappuram.

Our Journey

Milestones

2013

Evoca Fashion opened its first showroom in Cherpulassery, Palakkad.

2016

Expanded to Perinthalmanna, Malappuram with a dedicated bridal & groom wing.

2019

Launched the Pattambi store, introducing the signature Wedding Rental service.

2022

Opened the Koppam showroom and introduced All India Delivery.

2024

Celebrated 50,000+ satisfied customers with an expanded bespoke stitching unit.
